03. Projects

UPI Offline Mesh Source ↗ Case study ↗

Deferred Settlement System

Spring Boot backend simulating offline UPI-style payments relayed device-to-device through a Bluetooth-style mesh until internet is reached.

  • Hybrid RSA-2048 + AES-256-GCM encryption keeps payment data unreadable to intermediate relay nodes.
  • Idempotency via atomic compare-and-set on ciphertext hashes → exactly-once settlement, even across concurrent bridge nodes.
  • Concurrency tests covering duplicate delivery & tampered-payload rejection.
